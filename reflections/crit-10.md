# Crit 10 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The Netflix model. It looks beautiful, and I think it should be like that for a household.

The other one was realising that asking a housemate to change our PIN for us counts as the awkwardness we talked about, the one this app fixes. It makes it inconvenient to ask the other person, and if we need it now while they're busy, we have to wait. We'd basically be asking them to change our password for us just to say "bro, this bill is mine".

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

We added PINs, but before that I was wondering whether this would affect our "trust each other" policy. It turned out it wasn't damaging that. We just added a fix for the problem where we could log in as someone we didn't intend to log in as.

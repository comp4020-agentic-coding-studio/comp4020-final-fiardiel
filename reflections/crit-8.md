# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I was stuck at the start because the brief, a multi-user app, was too broad to start from. So I stopped thinking about apps and started from my house. I named the things that annoyed me: the kitchen, cleanliness and splitting costs. The agent pointed out that the kitchen is where the live problem (people colliding) and the slow problem (the mess) meet, and I chose the kitchen as the centre of the app. Choosing it gave the app a direction, which made building it easier, because I could then decide the rules for it.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

What I took from this is judging what is right and what is wrong in the agent's output, like the rule for the blame. I had decided that someone who starts cooking in a messy kitchen is not blamed for the mess. The agent's first written version of the rule would have blamed them, and the review caught it. Before I ask an agent to build something, I decide the rules myself and write them down, and then I check the agent's answer against those rules instead of taking its first answer.

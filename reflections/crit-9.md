# Crit 9 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The first breakthrough was switching the app from the kitchen to Serumah, because it is something close to home. The second was changing how payments work, from waiting for the person who was paid to tap "Got it" to counting straight away, once I remembered who the app is for. It's just for our house, and households like us.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Thinking about my house first, instead of the careful option, made me remember Robin Sloan's home-cooked app: we're building something tailored to our house's needs, so we made it simpler, for the house only.

I also changed my mind after something was already built. I would do that again, especially if the built version was doing the careful option instead of what the house needs.

When I asked the agent to write my README, it stopped at the rule in my `CLAUDE.md` that says the README is my writing. That mattered because the rules hold the overall idea. If the ground rules are broken, the implementation has drifted away from its ground ideas: either the idea was changed, or the implementation is wrong.
